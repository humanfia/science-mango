\#\# Physical scenario

with r << R, volume V, and cross-sectional area A.  An insulated conducting
wire is wound densely around it with N turns and instantaneous current I.
Fields H and B and magnetization M are approximately uniform in the torus.

heat entering the paramagnetic torus are positive.

\begin{theorem}[Ipho Physics formalization target]
\label{thm:physics:ipho_2026_t3_a2:target}
\leanok
For the densely wound paramagnetic torus in the thin-torus ($r \ll R$)
uniform-field model, the work performed by the external voltage source when
the magnitude of $\vec B$ changes by $dB$ is
\[
  dW_{emf} \;=\; V\,H\,dB,
\]
in terms of the torus volume $V$, the $H$-field magnitude $H$ and the field
change $dB$, as requested by T3-A2.  Lean:
\texttt{IPhO2026.T3A2.emf\_work} (packaged form
\texttt{IPhO2026.T3A2.emf\_work\_eq\_expr}); sign-convention coherence in
\texttt{IPhO2026.T3A2.emf\_work\_positive}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The azimuthal $\vec B$ is uniform and perpendicular to the tube cross-section,
so each of the $N$ turns links the flux $B \cdot A$ and the winding's total
flux linkage is $\lambda = N B A$ (\texttt{fluxLinkage}); a change $dB$ of the
field magnitude changes the linkage by $N\,A\,dB$
(\texttt{fluxLinkage\_change}).  Faraday's law in step form gives
$\varepsilon\,dt = N\,dB\,A$ for the voltage the source must supply against
the induced emf (\texttt{FaradaysLawWinding}), and the source maintaining the
instantaneous current $I$ performs $dW_{emf} = I\,\varepsilon\,dt$
(\texttt{SourceWorkStep}; negligible wire resistance, so no Joule term), hence
$dW_{emf} = I\,N\,A\,dB$ (\texttt{source\_work\_of\_linkage\_change}).
Ampère's law on the mean circle combined with the torus volume--geometry gives
$H\,V = N\,I\,A$ (\texttt{field\_times\_volume}), and substituting
$N I A = H V$ yields $dW_{emf} = (H V)\,dB = V H\,dB$ (\texttt{emf\_work}).
The T3-A1 result $H = NIA/V$ is re-derived inline from the problem-only
governing laws (\texttt{field\_magnitude\_T3A1}), per the
\texttt{derive\_inline\_from\_problem\_only\_material} policy; no sibling file
is imported.  Lean proof bodies are deferred (\texttt{sorry}) at the
autoformalize stage.
\end{proof}
