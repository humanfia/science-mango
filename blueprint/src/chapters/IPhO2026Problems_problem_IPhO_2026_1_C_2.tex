\begin{definition}[C.1 threshold photon energy $\hbar\omega_{\min}(\theta)$]
\label{def:IPhO2026Problems_problem_IPhO_2026_1_C_2:hbarOmegaMin}
\lean{IPhO2026_1_C_2.hbarOmegaMin}
The minimum photon energy required for dissociation at outgoing O$_2$ angle
$\theta \le \pi/2$, inherited from part C.1 as a natural-language
prerequisite and written (as an energy, keeping $\hbar$ outside the
quotient) as a function of the rest scale $mc^2$ and the gap $\Delta U$:
\[
\hbar\omega_{\min}(\theta) =
\frac{3 m c^2\Bigl(1 - \sqrt{1 - \tfrac{\Delta U}{3 m c^2}
\bigl(2\sin^2\theta + 1\bigr)}\Bigr)}{2\sin^2\theta + 1} .
\]
This is a formula over the physical parameters, not the numeric C.2 answer.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}[Derivation sketch]

with $T_{\mathrm{O_2}} = P^2/(4m)$, $T_{\mathrm{O}} = q^2/(2m)$, and momentum
conservation with the parent at rest, $p_\gamma = \hbar\omega/c = \vec P + \vec q$
at relative angle $\theta$, eliminate the O-atom momentum $q$; minimizing the
resulting one-parameter family $\hbar\omega(P)$ at fixed $\theta, \Delta U$ by
setting its derivative to zero gives the minimum-energy (threshold) condition,
whose solution is the displayed expression (the minimum is approached at
nearly collinear fragment emission).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Threshold-excess enclosure at generic scales]
\label{thm:IPhO2026Problems_problem_IPhO_2026_1_C_2:threshold_excess_enclosure}

lower-root branch, with `0 < ΔU`, `0 < mc²`, `0 < S` and the smallness
certificate `2·ΔU·S ≤ mc²`.  Then the threshold excess over the gap is
enclosed between the leading recoil coefficient `b = S·ΔU²/(6·mc²)` and

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
With `c := 3·mc²/S` the balance rewrites as `c·(F−ΔU) = F²/2`, i.e.
`(F−ΔU)·(2c−F) = F·ΔU`; the branch condition and the smallness certificate
give `F ≤ 2ΔU < c`, and bootstrapping the quotient
`F−ΔU = F·ΔU/(2c−F)` once against `F ≤ 2ΔU` and then against the
resulting bound yields `F−ΔU ≤ ΔU²/(2c−3ΔU) ≤ b·(1 + 3·B·(2ΔU))` with
`B := S/(6·mc²)`; the lower bound `F−ΔU = B·F² > B·ΔU² = b` is immediate
from `ΔU < F`.
\end{proof}
