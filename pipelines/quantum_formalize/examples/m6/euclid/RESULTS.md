The algorithm explicitly cancels binary leading terms, computes each remainder with sufficient finite fuel, and iterates Euclid. Its output is proved equal to the normalized gcd, including zero operands and constants. The remainder potential c+rank(r)≤rank(p) telescopes to at most rank(p)+rank(q) cancellations and at most rank(q) Euclidean rounds. Its dense shifted-XOR update, faithful fixed-width array representation, coefficient degree scan, and every intermediate operand's width safety are separately proved.
