# M6 pinned enumeration and exact minimum recovery — accepted

All 14 original frozen targets passed ordinary Lean compilation, independent exact-type and allowed-axiom audit, combined assembly, and unchanged-environment verification. The final experiment is distance-repair-rm3zf5mn; canonical evidence is experiment/ with a 260-file SHA256 manifest. This completes the generic pinned component, not the entire original M6 gate.

The accepted result identifies every integer enumerator coefficient with the exact number of full-weight vectors respecting the pins; establishes nonnegativity, free-bit partition and minimum-distance semantics; and proves correctness of the concrete zero-first recursive recovery. solve_exact returns none exactly when the logical set is empty. Otherwise its returned vector belongs to that set, has minimum physical Hamming weight, and uses at most m additional paired coefficient queries. At m=2N this is the original bound. Generic zero dimension, empty sets and pinned ones are retained. The final M6 root must instantiate the proved exact polynomial identity with actual transfer traces.

The initial live experiment accepted ten nodes. recover_properties needed two live attempts; the other accepted initial nodes passed their first attempts. enumerator_coeff had one compilation failure and its second round was interrupted by Physlib HTTP 500. Its pointwise polynomial simplification repair passed the original frozen target and the normal continuation. That continuation accepted eleven nodes; first_positive_distance then had one compilation failure in empty-enumerator/Boolean-range simplification and another second-round Physlib HTTP 500. Its narrow exact-target repair passed independent and normal acceptance. Neither node exhausted five proof rounds, and neither outage was bypassed.

The final run recompiled and audited all eleven previously accepted proof bodies unchanged, normally accepted the checked first-positive repair, and obtained genuine live first-attempt successes for solve_exact and minimum_witness. Every replay verified its original receipt, candidate/target hashes, rendered draft, dependency payload digest and portable_declaration. The original 214-file and subsequent 224-file failure archives remain under experiments/. The source definitions, target statements, compiler limits and accepted-axiom policy were unchanged throughout.

Mathlib is pinned to de5ce8a9a66a4aa68a9bdbb35b63a06d34d9ca11 with Lean 4.34.0-rc1.
