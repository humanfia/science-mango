2. Domain, encoding and recognized predicate.
Take N>=1 and two N-bit coefficient arrays encoding nonempty supports A,B in {0,...,N-1}, of the same cardinality w. Equivalently, canonical residue lists may be converted to these arrays; their O(N log(N+1))-bit encoding is covered by the bounds. N is explicit physical length: this is not a polynomial-in-log-N algorithm on succinctly described codes. An implementation front-end may separately return InvalidInput for malformed encodings, unequal or zero weights, or failed connectedness. The theorem's three outcomes concern valid inputs.

Choose a0 in A and b0 in B and verify gcd(N,{i-a0:i in A},{j-b0:j in B})=1. The differences from one anchor generate exactly the same subgroup as all within-block differences, since i-j=(i-a0)-(j-a0). This is the required connectedness criterion, independent of chosen anchors.

Put n=N+1 and L=min(N-1,floor(log2 n)), computing the logarithm by integer bit length. For epsilon in {0,1}, let (A_epsilon,B_epsilon) be (A,B) or (B,A). Define R_orb to be the minimum, over epsilon, units t modulo N, alpha in A_epsilon and beta in B_epsilon, of the largest standard residue in t(A_epsilon-alpha) union t(B_epsilon-beta). The logical acceptance class is F!=1 and R_orb<=L. All constants are fixed, particularly c=1. There is no promise of an optimal supplied order and no unrestricted span parameter in the final resource exponents.

3. Authoritative discovery and outputs.
Construct a=sum_{i in A}x^i, b=sum_{j in B}x^j, M=x^N+1. Compute F=gcd(a,b,M) by ordinary binary polynomial Euclid, without taking a radical. If F=1, return (NoLogical,F).

Otherwise enumerate (epsilon,t,alpha,beta) lexicographically, with the ranges in section 2, testing units using integer gcd. For N=1 use the unique residue automorphism, represented by t=0. Form A'=t(A_epsilon-alpha) and B'=t(B_epsilon-beta), reduce to standard residues, and test whether both lie in [0,L]. Both contain zero automatically. Retain the first passing tuple, its arrays a',b', and actual R=max(deg a',deg b'). If no tuple passes, return (Unrecognized,F).

For a passing tuple recompute F'=gcd(a',b',M), put f=deg F', and call the exact physical optimizer in sections 5–6 with a',b',R,f. It returns (d,z'). Undo the recorded physical permutation as in section 4 and return (Recognized,F,d,z,epsilon,t,alpha,beta). The returned original gcd is F; F' is an internal normalization datum.

Compatibility with translation enumeration is exact at the level of recognized predicates. A companion tuple (epsilon,t,r,s) produces supports tA_epsilon+r and tB_epsilon+s. If these contain zero, there are alpha in A_epsilon and beta in B_epsilon with r=-t alpha and s=-t beta modulo N. Conversely each anchor tuple supplies those translations. Thus the sets of passing presentations coincide. Lexicographic enumeration in the different tuple coordinates need not select the same first presentation, and no part of the optimizer requires that equality. The retained actual arrays and their associated tuple are passed together. The search does not assume that the lexicographically canonical recipe representative minimizes span.

4. All-order algebra and physical transport.
Work in S=GF(2)[x]/(M). Write D=im[h->(ah,bh)] and C=ker[(u,v)->bu+av]. Characteristic two and commutativity give D subset C. If F=gcd(a,b,M), f0=deg F and K=M/F, a Bezout identity F=pa+qb+rM proves ker(h->(ah,bh))=ker(h->Fh). Indeed simultaneous annihilation by a,b implies annihilation by F; the converse follows because F divides a,b. Polynomial cancellation gives M|Fh iff K|h. Representatives of degree less than N in this kernel are Kt with deg t<f0. Consequently every boundary has exactly 2^f0 input preimages and dim D=N-f0. The syndrome image is the ideal generated by a,b in S, namely (F), of dimension N-f0. Therefore dim C=N+f0, dim(C/D)=2f0, and C=D exactly when F=1. No step assumes square-freeness. This establishes k=2deg F at even orders as well as odd orders.

For the discovered presentation first exchange physical blocks if epsilon=1. Put r=-t alpha and s=-t beta and let sigma_t(h)=h(x^t) modulo M. The map on the exchanged physical blocks is P(u,v)=(x^r sigma_t(u),x^s sigma_t(v)). It is a coefficient permutation, and a'=x^r sigma_t(a_epsilon), b'=x^s sigma_t(b_epsilon). Its syndrome is b'P(u,v)_1+a'P(u,v)_2=x^(r+s)sigma_t(b_epsilon u+a_epsilon v). It sends a boundary (a_epsilon h,b_epsilon h) to (a' sigma_t(h),b' sigma_t(h)). Its inverse first removes the two translations, then applies sigma_{t inverse}, then undoes the block exchange. Thus it bijects C with C' and D with D', preserving coefficient Hamming weight exactly in both directions. In particular deg F'=deg F, although the gcd polynomials need not be equal. Connectedness is preserved because translations leave differences unchanged and multiplication by a unit preserves their generated subgroup. The discovered anchored presentation therefore satisfies M6's stated hypotheses, including R<N.

For the CSS convention here, define J(u,v)_i=(v_{-i},u_{-i}), indices modulo N. If D_Z denotes the other boundary space, D_Z=JD. Directly, the coefficient equations for orthogonality to J(ah,bh), for every h, are bu+av=0; hence C=(JD)^perp. J is an orthogonal weight-preserving involution and identifies the two cycle/boundary pairs. Their pure-type minimum nonboundary weights agree. A nontrivial mixed CSS logical has a nonboundary pure component whose support is contained in the mixed Pauli support; a pure minimum attains the lower bound. Minimization over C minus D is therefore exact physical quantum distance. No module-basis weight or scalar surrogate is substituted.

5. Exact physical optimizer interface.
In this section use the discovered a',b' and f=deg F'. A state m=(m1,...,mR) stores preceding input bits. An edge labelled h in {0,1} shifts memory to (h,m1,...,m_{R-1}) and emits alpha=a'_0 h+sum_{j=1}^R a'_j mj and beta=b'_0 h+sum_{j=1}^R b'_j mj in GF(2). At R=0 there is one state with two distinct labelled loops.

A closed length-N labelled walk corresponds bijectively to an indexed cyclic input sequence h_i. In one direction its states have m_{i,j}=h_{i-j}; in the other, periodically extend a closed walk and iterate the shift rule backwards to force this formula. Emissions are (a'h)_i,(b'h)_i. Thus traces count indexed inputs, without division by rotations.

A pin configuration specifies an allowed set E_q equal to {0,1}, {0} or {1} at every physical coordinate q. Define b_q(e)=1_{e in E_q} y^e and c_q(e)=sum_{z in E_q}(-1)^(ez)y^z. At layer i assign each labelled edge boundary weight b_{L,i}(alpha)b_{R,i}(beta) and character weight c_{R,-i}(alpha)c_{L,-i}(beta). Let T_i and U_i be their transfer matrices, adding weights of parallel labelled edges in a matrix entry. Products are ordered by i=0,...,N-1.

The optimizer computes B_E=2^(-f)tr(product_i T_i), C_E=2^(-N)tr(product_i U_i), and Q_E=C_E-B_E. These are exact integer polynomials. The boundary trace counts each boundary satisfying the physical pins with multiplicity 2^f, proving the B_E formula.

For the character formula, a finite binary subspace V satisfies sum_{q in V}(-1)^(q dot z)=|V| if z is in V^perp and zero otherwise: outside V^perp, translating by an element with dot product one pairs opposite signs. Apply this to V=JD'. Parameterizing its elements by J(a'h,b'h) gives 2^f preimages per element and |JD'|=2^(N-f), so the input-sum normalization is 2^(-N). Summing pinned physical monomials factors coordinatewise into the c_q factors above. The emission alpha at layer i occupies coordinate (R,-i) after J and beta occupies (L,-i), explaining both the exchange and the inversion in the character pins. Therefore C_E enumerates exactly the pinned physical cycles and
Q_E(y)=sum_{z in C' minus D', z satisfies E} y^wt(z).

In particular normalization is coefficientwise integral; intermediate character coefficients may be negative, while Q_E has nonnegative integer coefficients. Fixed-one pins retain their contribution to weight. No unproved scalar-to-physical transport is present.

To compute any trace, process each initial state separately. Initialize its polynomial to 1 and all other state polynomials to zero. Propagate through N layers along both labelled edges, multiplying by their degree-at-most-two polynomial and adding into the successor. Add the final initial-state entry to a trace accumulator. Store every degree from zero through 2N. This finite recurrence specifies the transfer calculation without a matrix-power oracle.

6. Distance and minimum-witness decoding.
With all pins free choose the least d for which [y^d]Q_E>0. Such a coefficient exists because f>0 and Q_E(1)=2^(N+f)-2^(N-f)>0. Visit coordinates L0,...,L(N-1),R0,...,R(N-1). Tentatively pin the next coordinate to zero and recompute [y^d]Q. Retain zero if this coefficient is positive; otherwise pin one. The exact partition identity Q_E=Q_{E,q=0}+Q_{E,q=1} preserves a positive number of weight-d completions. After all coordinates are pinned, the sole compatible vector z' belongs to C' minus D' and has weight d. At most 2N additional paired trace queries suffice. The original unpinned polynomial excludes every lighter nonboundary. Section 4 transports both this lower bound and the attaining vector to original physical coordinates.

For N=1 a valid input has a=b=1 and F=1, so it returns NoLogical. The transfer definition still handles R=0 correctly through its two labelled loops. If desired, deterministic final verification can compute bu+av, the coefficient weight and membership in D by binary elimination; these checks are absorbed by the bounds below. They are not a substitute for the enumerator's lower-bound proof.

7. Resource compatibility and one common bound.
All bounds are worst-case, with constants independent of N,w and unrecognized span. The anchor search has at most 2N|A||B|<=2N^3 trials, no more than the companion's 2N^3 translation trials. Each trial scans at most 2N support entries, performs standard O(log^2 n)-bit residue arithmetic, and constructs/tests two length-N arrays. Thus complete discovery, including complete failure, costs O(N^4 log^2 n), bounded by O(n^6). Integer unit tests, connectedness, input conversion and counters fit this bound. No factorization or stored orbit is needed. Binary polynomial Euclid on degrees at most N, including the original and transformed gcds, has the conservative O(n^3) bit bound. Search storage is reused.

On acceptance let S0=2^R<=n. One trace has S0 starts, N layers, at most 2S0 edges per layer and O(N) coefficients per polynomial: O(N^2 S0^2) coefficient operations. An edge polynomial has coefficient l1 norm at most four. From one start there are at most 2^i paths at depth i, so absolute path contributions, and partial sums bounded by their absolute contributions, are at most 8^i. Summing starts gives the safe bound S0 8^N. Each signed coefficient therefore needs O(N+log S0)=O(n) bits. Constant-degree multiplication, addition, exact power-of-two division and comparisons are charged at this bit size.

Consequently one trace costs O(n^3 S0^2) indexed-array bit work, and the initial query plus at most 2N pinned queries cost O(n^4 S0^2)<=O(n^6). Two reusable layers contain O(n S0) coefficients of O(n) bits, giving O(n^2 S0)<=O(n^3) bits. Trace accumulators, transitions, pins, support arrays and the witness fit this bound. Transition construction and all inverse residue permutations are also absorbed. Queries do not retain predecessor histories or simultaneous workspaces. Direct final verification costs polynomial binary elimination on O(N^2) entries and fits as well.

These are bounds for the specified indexed-array algorithm, not a Python interpreter guarantee. For a common unconditional sequential model, expand it into bit operations and store memory as tagged sequential records. The O(n^3)-bit data, record addresses, counters and scratch space fit O(n^4) bits. An indexed access can be simulated by scanning the entire tagged store and comparing its O(log n)-bit address, with copying and bookkeeping charged explicitly; even a conservative O(n^6) allowance per indexed bit operation covers these tasks. Simulating O(n^6) indexed bit operations therefore gives O(n^12) deterministic sequential bit time and O(n^4) bits of storage. This intentionally adopts a common conservative bound rather than identifying the standalone drafts' sharper sequential claims. It covers preprocessing, failed discovery, NoLogical, all successful optimization queries and transport. No uncontrolled parameter appears in its exponents.

8. Coverage and the precise non-Cartesian claims.
The principal non-Cartesian property here means that no allowed recipe transformation gives a separated-direction presentation a=A0(x^q), b=B0(x^m), where N=mq, m,q>=2 and gcd(m,q)=1. This is precisely the presentation type defined in the cited Cartesian manuscript, extended over the original permitted recipe orbit. It does not exclude arbitrary Clifford equivalence or arbitrary tensor descriptions of the quantum code.

For a support T define g(T)=gcd(N,{t-t0:t in T}). Translation preserves g and a unit multiplier preserves the subgroup generated by the differences, hence preserves g. A support in a coset of qZ/NZ has g divisible by q. Thus a block with g=1 cannot occupy a proper separated direction. This gives a simple invariant obstruction; it does not rely on block-product failure.

First, a=b=p=1+x+x^2 at every N=3t, t>=1, is admitted. Both supports have weight three and g=1, so connectedness and the separated-direction obstruction hold. Since p divides x^3+1 and hence x^N+1, F=p. The input polynomials themselves cap the multiplicity at one even when N is even. Identity presentation has R=2<=L for N>=3, so the authoritative search succeeds. These include infinitely many odd and even orders. They have d=2 with witness (1,1): it is a cycle; being a boundary would require ph=1, impossible because p is a nonunit; and any weight-one vector has nonzero syndrome, a monomial multiple of nonzero p.

Second, a=b=q0=1+x+x^2+x^3=(x+1)^3 at every even N>=8 is admitted and has g=1 in both blocks. Its identity span is three and passes L. Write N=2^v m with m odd. Since x^N+1=(x^m+1)^(2^v), and the derivative of x^m+1 is nonzero at 1, the multiplicity of x+1 is exactly 2^v. Hence F=(x+1)^min(3,2^v). This includes common multiplicity two for v=1 and three for v>=2, in particular the first draft's N=8m subfamily. The same argument gives d=2 and witness (1,1). These families are separated-direction-non-Cartesian and are covered without any square-free reduction.

Retain also the first draft's a=1+x, b=1+x^2 at every N>=7. It has equal weight two, connectedness from difference one, F=x+1 and identity span two, so it passes the same recognizer at odd and even orders. It is separated-direction-non-Cartesian because the first block has g=1. A distinct additional property holds: its cycle space is not U times V on the two physical blocks. Indeed (a,b) is a cycle; product structure would imply (a,0) is a cycle, whereas its syndrome ab=1+x+x^2+x^3 is nonzero modulo M for N>=7. This product-space property is invariant under the blockwise permutations and exchange in the recipe group. It is not being equated with the separated-direction property; both have their own proofs. At prime N there is additionally no factorization of the site count into two integers greater than one.

All these explicit families overlap M6's fixed-span domain. Their purpose is successful physical coverage and multiplicity control, not novelty beyond every earlier tractable regime. The consolidation adds complete orbit discovery and fixed polynomial charging for the N-dependent logarithmic cutoff; it does not claim a new M6 identity.

9. Explicit excluded family and M9 complement.
The exact surviving complement returned as Unrecognized is F!=1 and R_orb>L, with the identical predicate at even and odd orders. A sufficient failure condition is w>L+1, since an interval [0,L] has only L+1 sites.

For an explicit sparse connected excluded family take N=2^v>=8 and a=b=(1+x)(1+x^(N/2)), with support {0,1,N/2,N/2+1}. Both supports have weight four and contain a difference one. In characteristic two a=(1+x)^(N/2+1), so the full gcd is F=a!=1. Every unit is odd and sends the antipodal difference N/2 to itself modulo N. Translations preserve that difference. A support in [0,R] containing an antipodal pair must have R>=N/2, while L=v<N/2. Thus every authoritative discovery trial fails and the output is Unrecognized. These examples themselves have d=2 by the diagonal argument; rejection is neither a distance assertion nor evidence of hardness.

No completeness for other structural regimes or all favorable coordinate orders is asserted. Arbitrary-span optimization, unrestricted surviving scalar cores and family-preserving hardness remain M9 work. Nothing in this proof settles either general alternative, and neither is required for the revised M8 mathematical baseline.
