# Actual Record group and full-stabilizer counting

The actual `M7.Action.Record N` group uses the verified composition, identity and inverse laws of the literal translation/unit/exchange records. Its action is exactly `M7.Action.act` on pairs of finite supports. `N > 0` is represented by the existing `[NeZero N]` domain; no faithfulness premise is introduced, so the abstract exchange at order one remains a distinct action record.

`M7.ActualOrbit.fullStabilizer` checks every actual record fixing both supports. `actionCount`, `fiberCount`, `orbit` and `distinctCount` are explicit finite filters/images of the literal action. The six frozen targets specialize the verified generic fiber theorem, strict stabilizer positivity, multiplication law, exact division/divisibility and Boolean partitions. All six depend only on the imported accepted closures and may run independently. Imported statements, portable proof bodies, frozen targets, receipts and original compiled hashes are checked in `IMPORT_PROVENANCE.json`.

These semantic finite counts are proof-side interfaces. They do not replace the production factorized numerator evaluator, original prefix-completion arithmetic, coverage selector or M6 quality instance. Those bridges remain separate original-M7 integration obligations, not extra strengthened goals.

The group adapter requires `noncomputable section` because the existing finite-support action uses classical image equality. The original adapter and initial compilation evidence are preserved in `TYPE_REPAIR.json`; this changes no mathematical statement and makes no execution-efficiency claim.
