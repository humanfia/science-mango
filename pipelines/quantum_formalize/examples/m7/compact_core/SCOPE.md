# Actual emission and recurrence structure

Eight component targets on the original arithmetic generator: actual action/inverse, full signature transport, computed full stabilizer, checked descent transcript, finite recurrence length, stored-base inclusion and exact fold reconstruction, immediate stopping. Actual recovery, freshness, correct fuel exhaustion, full coverage and original resources remain downstream obligations. No free transversal or distance oracle is introduced. The root count is cached once by generate and recomputed once per insertion.
