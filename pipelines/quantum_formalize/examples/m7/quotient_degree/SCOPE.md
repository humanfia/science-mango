# Full polynomial quotient cardinality

Five exact targets connect the actual quotient by a monic divisor F to AdjoinRoot F, prove cardinality 2^natDegree(F), and derive degree invariance under an induced ideal automorphism. Degree-zero, even N and repeated factors are retained. The final signature transport must supply the actual unit substitution automorphism; this intermediate theorem is not a complete M7 root.
