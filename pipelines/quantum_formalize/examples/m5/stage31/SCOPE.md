# Original residue occurrence formula A (source §4)

`rawA T w F` is exactly the finite sum over positive divisors d of T and subsets S of the distinct residual factors of M_T/F of `mu(d) * (-1)^|S| * ROne(F*product(S),T,d,w-1)^2`. ROne is the accepted character-power arithmetic R at quotient target 1, with a total monicity wrapper. `A w F` sets T to the accepted signature period. Neither arithmetic definition enumerates tuples or support pairs.

The semantic objects are two tails `Fin (w-1) → Fin T`. Their anchored polynomial is explicitly `1 + sum X^(tail_i)`, retaining repeated coordinates and all binary cancellations. Their integer gcd is exactly `gcd(T, gcd(univ.gcd(val_r), univ.gcd(val_s)))`; it is not computed from polynomial support after cancellation. The implicit first residue is zero. The later stage32 Fin.cons head-tail correspondence connects this representation to the full anchored Fin w tuples used in stage28; this preparation does not claim that correspondence or add an assumption for it.

The generalized counting identity assumes only T>0, w>0, monic F, and F dividing M_T. Its period specialization uses monic F with constant coefficient 1. There is no weight upper bound, injectivity requirement, squarefree assumption, or nonzero-block hypothesis. Empty tails (w=1), F=1, repeated residues, and zero residue polynomials remain included. Positivity is equivalent to existence of feasible tails; the packing/source construction is a subsequent use of this exact finite count.

The six targets are the R-square pair count, the combined pair indicator, arithmetic sum expansion, exact rawA count, nonnegativity/positive-iff-existence, and the original period-A specialization. The indicator can be tackled independently. The R-square helper and downstream arithmetic assembly use the now accepted, receipt-verified stage29 restricted_R_count and its exact proof closure. The earlier pending gate is retained in the before-import graph and preflight snapshots. Stage27 has no accepted generic helper to import and is not a dependency of this batch.

Factor-subset indexing is sufficient finite factor arithmetic for the original M5 objective. Literal squarefree-divisor reindexing is optional representation correspondence, not an extra acceptance gate. The formulas use the existing noncomputable quotient-coordinate representation; no executable algorithm refinement is claimed by this batch.
