# Explicit anchor from a residue tail

The source stores a length-w tuple with first coordinate0; arithmetic A naturally counts a length-(w-1) tail with an implicit0. Fin.cons0 turns the tail into the exact full tuple used by the support construction. Three targets prove its zero anchor, polynomial1+tail-sum identity, and preservation of the integer gcd. Repetition and empty tails are allowed; no supplied physical witness is assumed.
