# Arbitrary selected-polynomial tuple completion

The selected polynomial Z includes the implicit anchor and all chosen residues in the global conditional formula. This block identity allows arbitrary Z, including0: R evaluated at mk(Z) counts length-k completions for which Z plus the new tuple polynomial is divisible by P. Divisor-restricted residue choices are transported to Fin T with an explicit divisibility filter.

Arithmetic R remains the accepted character-power definition. Global prefix lengths, selected gcd conditions, and the two-block conditional A formula are separate integration obligations; no selected tuple distinctness or squarefree restriction is added.
