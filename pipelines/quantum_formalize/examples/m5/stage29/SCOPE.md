# Anchored divisor-restricted tuple count

R(P,T,d,k,1) counts tails of length k with an implicit anchored0, hence weight k+1. Divisibility of1 plus the tail polynomial is equivalent to its quotient residue being1. For positive T and d dividing T, the finite residue domain d times Fin(T/d) is equivalent to the residues in Fin T divisible by d. This transports R to the exact filtered tuple domain used in A.

R remains the arithmetic character-power definition; semantic tuple-filter counts occur only on theorem right-hand sides. Repetition, cancellation, zero residue polynomials and length0 remain allowed. This stage does not assert the full A formula, necessity or recovery.
