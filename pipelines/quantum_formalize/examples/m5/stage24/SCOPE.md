# Arithmetic repeated-residue count

R is the source character-power formula divided by 2^degree(P). Its definition enumerates characters and single residues, not tuple pairs. The exact-count theorem identifies it with ordered tuples with repetition from d times Fin(T/d). Original use has positive T and d dividing T; the algebraic identity needs no additional restriction. It includes k=0, empty index sets and P=1. The definition remains noncomputable through existing quotient coordinates; executable refinement and assembly into A remain open.

Imports are the immutable audited 90-theorem checkpoint. Four targets connect polynomial tuple sums to coordinates, prove the numerator identity, cancel its denominator, and conclude nonnegativity.
