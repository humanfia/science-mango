# Residue branching with counted tests

pick scans candidate residues and explicitly records the number of count tests. recover appends a positive-count residue and structurally recurses on the remaining coordinate count. Four targets show search soundness, at-most-list-length cost, successful positive reconstruction, and terminal validity with total cost at most m*T.

The count-partition and terminal-validity premises must later be discharged by the exact arithmetic conditional A formula. This generic theorem is an algorithm component, not a replacement for that arithmetic correctness obligation. Instantiating m=2(w-1) gives the original candidate-test bound; no faster complexity goal is introduced.
