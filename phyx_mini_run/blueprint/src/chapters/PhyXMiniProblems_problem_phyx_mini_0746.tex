\#\# Physical scenario

A skilled skier knows to jump upward before reaching a downward slope. Consider a jump in which the launch speed is \$v\_0 = 10\textbackslash{} m/s\$, the launch angle is \$\textbackslash{}theta\_0 = 11.3\textasciicircum{}\textbackslash{}circ\$, the initial course is approximately flat, and the steeper track has a slope of \$9.0\textasciicircum{}\textbackslash{}circ\$. Figure shows a prejump that allows the skier to land on the top portion of the steeper track. Figure 4-42b shows a jump at the edge of the steeper track. In figure, the skier lands at approximately the launch level.

\#\# Question

How far below the launch level does the skier land?

\#\# Answer choices

- A: A: 0.92 m

- B: B: 1.0 m

- C: C: 1.11 m

- D: D: 1.23 m
