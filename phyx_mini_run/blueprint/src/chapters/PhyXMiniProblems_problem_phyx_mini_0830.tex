\begin{theorem}[Physics formalization target]
\label{thm:physics:phyx_mini_0830:target}
\lean{PhyXMiniProblems.ProblemPhyXMini0830.meet_two_hundred_forty_miles_east_of_chicago}
\uses{def:physics:phyx-mini-0830:phyxminiproblems-problemphyxmini0830-twotravelermeetingsetup, def:physics:phyx-mini-0830:phyxminiproblems-problemphyxmini0830-matchestravelscenario, def:physics:phyx-mini-0830:phyxminiproblems-problemphyxmini0830-matchesproblemreadouts, def:physics:phyx-mini-0830:phyxminiproblems-problemphyxmini0830-matchessuppliedfigure, def:physics:phyx-mini-0830:phyxminiproblems-problemphyxmini0830-hasphysicalmeetingparameters, def:physics:phyx-mini-0830:phyxminiproblems-problemphyxmini0830-satisfieszeroaccelerationlaw, def:physics:phyx-mini-0830:phyxminiproblems-problemphyxmini0830-satisfiesuniformmotionlaw, def:physics:phyx-mini-0830:phyxminiproblems-problemphyxmini0830-ismeetingevent, def:physics:phyx-mini-0830:phyxminiproblems-problemphyxmini0830-meetingdisplacementeastofchicagoinmiles}
With east positive, Bob starts at Chicago and drives at
$60\,\mathrm{mi/h}$ while Susan starts $400\,\mathrm{mi}$ east of Chicago
and drives at $-40\,\mathrm{mi/h}$.  Their designated meeting point is
$240\,\mathrm{mi}$ east of Chicago, answer B.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take Chicago as mile zero and let
$\tau=t_{\mathrm{meet}}-9$ be the positive number of hours after the common
departure.  Uniform motion gives Bob's position as $60\tau$ miles and
Susan's as $400-40\tau$ miles.  The meeting predicate makes these positions
equal, so $60\tau=400-40\tau$.  Hence $\tau=4$ and their common position is
$60\cdot4=240$ miles.  Subtracting Chicago's zero coordinate therefore gives
a meeting displacement of $240\,\mathrm{mi}$ to the east.
\end{proof}
