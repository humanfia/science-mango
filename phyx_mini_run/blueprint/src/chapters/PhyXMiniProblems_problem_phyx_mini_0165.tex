\#\# Physical scenario

The ``lens'' on a camera is usually a combination of two or more single lenses. Consider a camera in which light passes first through a diverging lens, with \$f\_1 = -120\textbackslash{};mm\$, then a converging lens, with \$f\_2 = 42\textbackslash{};mm\$, spaced \$60\textbackslash{};mm\$ apart. A reasonable definition of the effective focal length of this lens combination is the focal length of a single lens that could produce an image in the same location if placed at the midpoint of the lens combination. A \$10\textbackslash{};cm\$-tall object is \$500\textbackslash{};mm\$ from the first lens.

\#\# Question

What is the effective focal length of the double-lens system used in this camera?
