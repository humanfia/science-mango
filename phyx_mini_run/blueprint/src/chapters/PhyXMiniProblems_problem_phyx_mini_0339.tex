The internal-energy change along the displayed isothermal ideal-gas process is zero joules.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The figure predicate states that every point of the process, hence both endpoints, has temperature $85^\circ$C.  The caloric law makes internal energy a function only of absolute temperature, so the endpoint internal energies agree.  Their difference, which is the defined internal-energy change, is therefore zero.
\end{proof}
