\#\# Physical scenario

Consider three spin-1/2 particles arranged on the corners of a triangle and interacting via the Hamiltonian \$\$H = J (\textbackslash{}mathbf\{S\}\_1 \textbackslash{}cdot \textbackslash{}mathbf\{S\}\_2 + \textbackslash{}mathbf\{S\}\_2 \textbackslash{}cdot \textbackslash{}mathbf\{S\}\_3 + \textbackslash{}mathbf\{S\}\_3 \textbackslash{}cdot \textbackslash{}mathbf\{S\}\_1),\$\$ where \$J\$ is a positive constant. This interaction favors opposite alignment of neighboring spins (\textbackslash{}textbf\{antiferromagnetism\}, if they are magnetic dipoles), but the triangular arrangement means that this condition cannot be satisfied simultaneously for all three pairs. This is known as geometrical 'frustration'.
