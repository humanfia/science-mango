Relativistic addition of \(0.900c\) and \(0.700c\) gives the probe's
Earth-frame speed \(160c/163\), which rounds to \(0.982c\), answer D.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Substitute the two normalized velocities into the collinear addition law:
\[
 \beta=\frac{9/10+7/10}{1+(9/10)(7/10)}
      =\frac{16/10}{163/100}=\frac{160}{163}.
\]
The denominator is positive.  Direct rational comparison gives
\(0.9815\le160/163<0.9825\), the declared nearest-thousandth interval about
answer D.
\end{proof}
