Determine the number of all possible arrangements of the functional groups
on a hexadifferentiated α-CD (assume only the CH2OH groups have been mod-
ified).
SOLUTION:
If we have 6 linear positions for 6 coloured balls, the total number of arrange-
ments will be 6! = 720. But in α-CD, positions are in a circle, which makes all
arrangements below equal. The correct answer 6!/6 = 120.
