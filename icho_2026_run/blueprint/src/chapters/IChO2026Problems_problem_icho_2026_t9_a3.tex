\begin{theorem}[IChO 2026 T9-A3: ring size and stereocentre count]
\label{thm:physics:icho_2026_t9_a3:target}
\lean{IChO2026.T9.A3.icho_2026_t9_a3}
\leanok
For the seven-repeat macrocycle $X$, the ring contains $35$ atoms and $X$
has $21$ stereocentres.  Each repeat contributes five ring atoms and three
stereocentres, including one pseudochiral acetal carbon; the complete
macrocycle is achiral.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Proved in full.  Ring membership is decided by graph connectivity: an
explicit 35-edge closed walk through `C4–C5–O5–C1–OGly` of each of the seven
units is shown to be a cycle (`ringCycle_isCycle`), so every
`{C1, O5, C5, C4, OGly}` atom is a ring atom; the pendant hydroxymethyl and
hydroxyl atoms are excluded by leaf pruning (a cycle vertex has two distinct
neighbors, via `IsCycle.snd_ne_penultimate`, and rotation moves any cycle
vertex to the start).  Stereogenicity uses the automorphism definition: C4
and C5 have pairwise distinct ligand labels, and at the acetal carbon C1 the
two ring-oxygen ligands cannot be swapped by any reference-label-preserving
automorphism because the swap would force the oppositely labelled C4/C5
branches to coincide (`not_ligandEquivalent_C1_O5_OGly`), while the
configuration-blind reflection about C1 does swap them
(`reflectionAutomorphism`), making C1 pseudochiral.  The per-unit counts
(5 ring atoms, 3 stereocentres, 1 pseudochiral centre) follow by Finset
enumeration, achirality from the reflection inverting every true-centre
label, and `rs = 7 · 5 = 35`, `sc = 7 · 3 = 21` by the disjoint-union
bookkeeping sums.
\end{proof}
