\begin{theorem}\leanok
[Icho Chemistry formalization target]
\label{thm:physics:icho_2026_t5_a3:target}
\lean{IChO2026Problems.T5A3.fatty_acid_formula_from_ozonolysis_and_bond_total}
The ozonolysis products and unsaturation count determine the molecular formula
and total bond count of the fatty acid.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

\leanok
Three ozonolysis products and the cleavage law give $N_{C=C}=2$.  The
hydrocarbon valence balance is then $y=2x+1-2N_{C=C}=2x-3$.  Substitution into
the supplied non-ionised-PL1 bond count gives $4x+y=99$, hence
$6x-3=99$, so $x=17$ and $y=31$.  Adding the carboxyl group gives
$\mathrm{C}_{18}\mathrm{H}_{32}\mathrm{O}_2$; the prior visual part is not
used as a Lean premise.
\end{proof}
