# A proof gap does not require disproving all available methods

This clarifies the user's instruction not to strengthen proof targets. Read it with docs/M5_ORIGINAL_STRENGTH_GUARD_20260914.md; original M5 is unchanged.

A coverage audit may identify an unproved logical implication by stating the quantified conclusion required by original M5, the hypotheses and outputs of the supplied theorems, and the missing inference between them. It does NOT have to exhibit an admissible sector and prove that every existing construction or every choice of witness fails on that sector. Such an impossibility theorem would be an additional, stronger mathematical task.

Likewise, a task to determine the remaining proof obligations does NOT also have to discover a new theorem as a condition of success. A precise coverage ledger is a valid scoped audit output. An independently proved new sufficient construction is a valid scoped theorem even if the author cannot prove that it strictly enlarges the union of all historical construction domains. Novelty and proof of strict separation are not added M5 acceptance gates.

Keep the two claims distinct:

- The supplied proof cover has not established a required quantified conclusion.
- There exists a sector that no supplied construction can produce.

The first does not require proving the second. Neither alone proves that the target sector is impossible. A failed sufficient bound also remains only a failed bound. Do not hide unknown cases or announce full M5 merely because no counterexample is known.

For the next task, compose the actually proved sector-generation implications, identify the least explicit logical step still required if any, and assess any genuine completion argument on its merits. No counterexample to all methods, strict novelty theorem, all-fibre enumeration, anchored optimization, inherited variant, or additional weight-generalization is required as an extra outcome. Preserve every already proved result and every viable proof route.
