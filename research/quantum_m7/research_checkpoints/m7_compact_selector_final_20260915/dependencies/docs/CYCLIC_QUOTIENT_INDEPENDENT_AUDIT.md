## 2. The gap this audit was designed to close

The constructor's only pair-level brute force,
`test_canonical_form_equals_full_declared_orbit_minimum`, enumerates each orbit
by sweeping the parameterization

```text
unit -> optional block exchange -> independent translations
```

which is exactly the parameterization its canonicalizer minimizes over. If the
group generated by the declared operations contained an element outside that
normal form, the constructor's canonicalizer and the constructor's brute force
would both miss it and would still agree. The comparison is therefore
consistent by construction rather than independent. That test also covers only
`N=5, w=2`: prime order, cyclic unit group of prime order, and no support with
a nontrivial translation stabilizer. The only periodic-support test in the
constructor's suite acts on a single support, never on a pair.

The audit removes both weaknesses.

- **Orbits are computed by breadth-first closure under the declared generators
  alone** — translation by one in each block, block exchange, and every unit
  multiplier. This assumes nothing about a normal form, so a group element
  outside the constructor's parameterization would split a `kappa` fibre and be
  detected by check A2.
- **The translation normal form is minimized over all `N` shifts**, not over the
  `w` support anchors, so the constructor's anchor shortcut becomes an audited
  claim rather than a shared assumption.
- **Ten domains** were preregistered, spanning even orders, non-square-free
  orders, non-cyclic unit groups, weights 2 through 5, and pair-level periodic
  supports.

## 3. Proof-level lemmas the constructor's proof used implicitly

The written proof of part (2) says that equality of canonical forms "supplies an
explicit unit, optional swap, and two translations". That step is valid only
because the minimized set covers the whole group. The audit supplies the two
missing lemmas explicitly and then verifies both computationally.

**Normal-form lemma.** In `G_N` the relations

```text
M_u T_{s,t} = T_{us,ut} M_u,      S T_{s,t} = T_{t,s} S,      S M_u = M_u S
```

hold, so every word in the generators rewrites as `T_{s,t} S^e M_u`, and

```text
|G_N| = N^2 phi(N) 2.
```

Verified as check A9 for `N in {5,6,8,9}`: the parameter set has the stated
cardinality, contains every declared generator, is closed under the composition
rule

```text
(u2,e2,s2,t2) o (u1,e1,s1,t1)
  = (u2 u1, e1 xor e2, s2 + sigma_0, t2 + sigma_1),
    (sigma_0,sigma_1) = (u2 s1, u2 t1) swapped iff e2 = 1,
```

and that rule agrees with literal function composition on sampled words. Checks
A9 on the five full-sweep domains additionally confirm that the normal-form
sweep partition is identical to the assumption-free generator-closure
partition.

**Independent-shift lemma.** Minimizing the ordered pair
`(A+s, B+t)` lexicographically decouples, because the first component depends
only on `s` and the second only on `t`; hence the joint minimum is
`(nu(A), nu(B))`.

**Anchor lemma.** The lexicographically least translate of a nonempty support
has least residue `0`, because some translate does and any tuple beginning with
`0` precedes any tuple beginning with a positive residue. Hence minimizing over
the `w` anchors equals minimizing over all `N` shifts. Verified exhaustively on
every support of every audit domain and on all `binom(105,3) = 187,460` supports
of `C_105`, with zero mismatches.

## 5. Negative controls

A green audit is only informative if the checks can fail. Four deliberately
wrong inputs were passed through the same machinery on D1-D6; each was detected.

| control | mutation | detected on |
|---|---|---|
| NC1 | canonical map with the unit minimization deleted | D1-D6 |
| NC2 | canonical map with block exchange deleted | D2-D6 |
| NC3 | acting group enlarged by *independent* unit multipliers | D1-D6 |
| NC4 | acting group shrunk by deleting block exchange | D2-D6 |

NC3 matters most for scope. Allowing independent units `(A,B) -> (uA, vB)`
strictly coarsens the quotient — on `N=9, w=3` it collapses 12 orbits to 6, on
`N=15, w=2` it collapses 10 to 6 — and the true `kappa` is then non-constant on
those enlarged orbits. So the declared group is strictly smaller than that
natural larger candidate, and `kappa` is not vacuously constant. NC4 shows it
is not vacuously injective either: deleting block exchange refines `N=9, w=3`
from 12 orbits to 20, and `kappa` then assigns one value to two distinct
orbits. Independent units are correctly excluded from the declared group,
because the `X`-check rows are shared by the two blocks and so cannot absorb
two different site relabelings.

## 6. Code soundness, machine-checked

The theorem note argues in prose that each declared operation is a CSS
permutation equivalence. The audit turns that into 52 explicit certificates
over `N in {5,7,9,15}`, including the periodic supports `{0,3,6}` in `C_9` and
`{0,5,10}` in `C_15`. For a word `x` and a group element `(u,e,s,t)`, the
certificate exhibits

```text
qubit map      pi(beta, j)   = (beta xor e, u^{-1} (j - s_beta)),
X-check map    rho_X(r)      = u^{-1} r,
Z-check map    rho_Z(r)      = u^{-1} (r - s - t),
```

and verifies, as GF(2) matrix equality on explicitly built `105`-style
circulant blocks, that `H_X` and `H_Z` of the image word are the row- and
column-permuted originals. The single most important point, checked
explicitly, is that **one and the same qubit permutation `pi` works for both
Pauli sectors**; a construction needing different qubit permutations for `X`
and `Z` would not be a code equivalence at all. `H_X H_Z^T = 0` was verified on
both sides of every certificate.

## 7. `C105` reconstruction

Working only from `evidence/c105_recipe_orbit_certificates.jsonl`:

- **C1** all 2,544 stored members recanonicalize under the audit implementation
  to the declared 56 representatives; no member appears in two classes; every
  representative is its own canonical form.
- **C2** independently recomputed residual `U(105)` orbits and stabilizers
  reproduce the strata exactly: 50 classes with orbit 48 and stabilizer 1, six
  classes with orbit 24 and stabilizer 2. The stored member set of each class
  equals the recomputed residual orbit. All classes have
  `deg gcd(a,b,z^105-1) = 5` and connectivity index 1.
- **C3** all 2,544 witnesses stored by the constructor replay forward and
  reconstruct backward under the auditor's own action code, which was written
  from the operation-order specification rather than copied.
- **C4** for four preregistered classes the raw `G_105` orbit was built by
  literally sweeping all 1,058,400 group elements. Orbit-stabilizer held in
  every case:

  | representative | raw orbit | raw stabilizer |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | `([0,1,5],[0,1,5])` | 529,200 | 2 |
  | `([0,1,5],[0,1,26])` | 1,058,400 | 1 |
  | `([0,1,5],[0,5,64])` | 529,200 | 2 |
  | `([0,1,5],[0,11,76])` | 529,200 | 2 |

  This records a distinction worth keeping straight. The constructor's
  "residual unit stabilizer" is the stabilizer in `U(105)` of the already
  swap-and-translation-quotiented pair; the raw stabilizer is the stabilizer in
  `G_105` of the ordered raw word. They genuinely differ: the diagonal word
  `([0,1,5],[0,1,5])` has residual unit stabilizer of size 1 but raw stabilizer
  of size 2, the extra element being block exchange. Both orbit-stabilizer
  identities hold, each in its own group.
- **C5** the anchor shortcut equals the unrestricted translation minimum on all
  187,460 three-subsets of `C_105`. The 1,786 translation classes have sizes
  `1785 x 105` and `1 x 35`; the single short class is the periodic support
  `{0,35,70}`, and the class sizes sum to `binom(105,3)`.

## 8. Addendum C6: generator surjectivity at the `C105` fibre

The theorem note explicitly left open that "the catalogue generator enumerates
every admissible word". The auditor re-enumerated the admissible set from
scratch: all `binom(105,3)` supports, their translation normal forms, all
1,595,791 unordered pairs of normal forms, filtered by the auditor's own binary
polynomial Euclid degree test and its own connectivity test. The result is
**exactly** the 2,544 stored words: zero missing, zero extra.

This closes surjectivity for this one fibre and this one constraint tuple. It
does not establish surjectivity for arbitrary requested constraints, which
remains open.

## 9. What the audit does *not* establish

- The quotient is by the **declared recipe group only**. Arbitrary qubit
  permutations, stabilizer row equivalence, local Clifford equivalence,
  entangling circuits, ancillas, and code deformation are all outside it. Two
  distinct canonical words can still present equivalent stabilizer codes; the
  audit says nothing about that and neither does the theorem.
- Nothing here is a statement about distance, a predictive quality law, or
  optimal selection.
- The uniform claim is a theorem about the quotient map for every `(N,w)`. The
  computational part of this audit is a finite exhaustive instantiation on ten
  domains plus `C105`; it corroborates the uniform proof but does not replace
  it, and no all-parameter statement is inferred from the finite catalogues.
- C6 covers one fibre and one constraint tuple.

- **G1** — complete canonical reduction theorem: closed. The reduction theorem

- **G2** — exact equivalence quotient: the recipe-equivalence portion is closed.
