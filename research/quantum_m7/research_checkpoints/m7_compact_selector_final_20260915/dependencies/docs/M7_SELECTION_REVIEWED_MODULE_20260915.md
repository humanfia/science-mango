# M7 reviewed module, not full milestone acceptance

Claim and argument reproduced verbatim from call-005 in run-efc8r824. Subsequent dual reviews found no gap in this module; review votes are not proof premises.

## Claim

Universal exact selection module over a complete recipe transversal. For every finite N>=1 and positive equal support weight w, given a complete transversal T of connected cyclic two-block binary CSS recipes under independent translations, one common unit multiplier and block exchange, the algorithm below terminates and returns exactly every optimal class and every requested optimal placement for the specified query language. It computes full-multiplicity k and exact physical quantum distance, constructs minimum logical witnesses, preserves ties, and certifies empty answers. Literal-signature queries have explicit orbit-intersection semantics and realizing witnesses. This establishes the assigned selection-module theorem, conditional on the explicitly supplied complete transversal; it does not establish the complementary generator, an integrated original-M7 completion, or fresh dual reviews.

## Argument

1. Source checks, scope, and changes.
All eight dependencies below were read in full, and their SHA-256 values were computed locally and matched their entries in _snapshot.json. No research code, tests, network access or agents were used. Numerical catalogue results and review verdicts are not premises. The M6 mathematical interface used here is its self-contained argument §§2–6 and §9, checked below. No SELF lemma, weight-three gap formula, or M8 assumption is used.

This is a new all-order selection module. Compared with the historical selector, it replaces unavailable catalogue labels by the exact M6 transfer calculation; makes signature transport explicit; distinguishes optimal classes from optimal physical placements; and preserves placement ties even when block-cost domination collapses to equality after taking a maximum. The historical catalogue supplies only a scope boundary, not universal labels or completeness.

2. Input interface and precise query language.
Let U(N) denote the permutations i↦ui of Z/NZ induced by units, with the sole identity permutation when N=1. Inputs are N>=1, w>=1, and a finite list T of canonical anchored pairs c=(A,B), each of weight w, containing exactly one representative of every connected recipe orbit. Connectedness means that the subgroup generated by all within-support differences is Z/NZ. For anchored pairs this is gcd(N,A,B)=1. If w>N, the legal recipe domain is empty and the answer is immediately empty.

The completeness of T is a mathematical premise, not inferred from its length, hashes, labels, or successful canonicalization. For end-to-end certificate replay, the generator must additionally provide a certificate Γ and a terminating sound verifier V_gen establishing exactly this premise. That generator and verifier are outside this sublemma. A sector-specific transversal is also sufficient if its certificate establishes exactly all orbits meeting the sector condition below; otherwise use the complete connected transversal.

A query consists of: a set K⊆{0,...,2N} of allowed logical dimensions; either all signatures or an explicitly listed finite set E of monic divisors of M=x^N+1; an optional integer distance floor D; optional nonnegative integer locality caps ℓ and r; and an ordered list of objective coordinates selected from L, R, -k and -d. The comparison is either componentwise Pareto minimization or lexicographic minimization in that list. An empty coordinate list is permitted and makes every feasible record tied. Integer input sets, divisibility and bounds are checked exactly. The default objectives are (L,R). A finite collection of orders/weights is handled by a disjoint union of these constructions, with N and w retained in every identifier.

The sector condition is existential: a class passes iff some member of its recipe orbit has gcd(a,b,M) in E. The answer includes a member witnessing this fact. This condition does not require every returned physical placement to have that literal signature; the sector witness and placement witness are distinct fields. If one instead requests that each physical placement have a signature in E, use the optional placement-sector mode: retain only placements satisfying that condition. The proof below covers both modes and never silently substitutes one for the other.

Distance is a tagged value: either an integer d or NoLogical. If a floor D or objective -d is requested, NoLogical records are infeasible. Otherwise k=0 records are allowed when 0∈K, and no numerical distance is assigned to them. Thus no convention of infinite logical distance enters an objective or feasibility test.

Additional predicates or objective coordinates can be added only as explicitly specified total computable functions with sound, terminating evaluation verifiers and, for class functions, a proof of invariance. Their evaluation and comparison costs must be added to the bounds below. The default k, distance, signature and locality evaluations are algorithms proved here, not oracles.

3. Exact group, all-order canonicalization and signature transport.
Write a group operation in normal form as g=T_(s,t) S^ε M_u. The identities M_u T_(s,t)=T_(us,ut) M_u, S T_(s,t)=T_(t,s) S and S M_u=M_u S show that these forms cover every word in the declared generators. All are invertible. There are 2φ(N)N² action records, although distinct records can act identically on a particular recipe.

For a nonempty support, its lexicographically least translate contains zero, since a translate containing zero precedes every translate whose least residue is positive. Consequently ν(A)=min_(a∈A) sorted(A-a). Independent shifts minimize the two components independently. Taking the minimum over units and swaps therefore gives κ, with κ(x)=κ(y) exactly when x,y belong to the same declared orbit. Every minimum records an invertible action. This argument uses only finite supports and permutations; it works at even N, with periodic supports, and at w=1. It does not use square-freeness.

For literal signatures work in S=GF(2)[x]/(M). The substitution σ_u:x^i↦x^(ui mod N) is a ring automorphism, with inverse σ_(u^-1). The ideal generated by a and b in S is the ideal generated by F=gcd(a,b,M), by polynomial Bézout. Translations multiply a and b by units of S and hence preserve this ideal; a swap also preserves it. A common unit sends it to the ideal generated by σ_u(F). Its unique monic divisor-of-M generator is
τ_u(F)=gcd(M, σ_u(F)).
Here σ_u(F) means substitution followed by reduction modulo M. Thus gcd(M,σ_u(a),σ_u(b))=τ_u(F), including every multiplicity. This is an ideal equality in the possibly nonreduced ring S, not an argument using only roots. The ideal (F) has GF(2)-dimension N-deg F, and σ_u is a vector-space bijection, so deg τ_u(F)=deg F. Also τ_vτ_u=τ_(vu), by uniqueness of the monic ideal generator.

It follows that testing τ_u(F)∈E for all u is necessary and sufficient for the existential sector query. Translations and swaps need not be scanned for this test. A successful u gives the explicit realizing member (uA,uB). The verifier recomputes its literal gcd as well as the transported signature. A canonical representative with F∉E is retained whenever one of its transported signatures belongs to E. This prevents the canonical-filter omission identified in the task.

4. All-order labels and physical witnesses: checked M6 interface.
For an anchored c, put R_c=max(deg a,deg b)<N and f=deg gcd(a,b,M). On S² define L(h)=(ah,bh), B=im L and C=ker[(u,v)↦bu+av]. Then B⊆C. Bézout gives ker L=ker multiplication by F. Writing M=FK, polynomial cancellation gives M|Fh iff K|h. There are exactly 2^f such representatives h of degree <N, so dim B=N-f and every boundary has 2^f preimages. No square-free assumption occurs.

The coordinate involution J(u,v)_i=(v_-i,u_-i) is orthogonal and weight preserving. With the stated circulant convention it sends X boundaries to Z boundaries, giving C=(JB)^⊥, dim C=N+f and k=2f. It interchanges the X and Z cycle/boundary pairs. These conclusions remain valid with repeated factors.

For completeness, the exact distance calculation is as follows. A memory of R_c preceding bits, with most recent bit first, receives t and outputs α=a_0t+Σ_j a_jm_j and β=b_0t+Σ_j b_jm_j in GF(2), then shifts. At R_c=0 retain both labeled loops. Indexed cyclic binary inputs h correspond bijectively to closed length-N labeled walks: the shift relation forces m_(i,j)=h_(i-j). There is no division by cyclic rotations.

Let T have edge weight y^(α+β), and U have edge weight (1+y)^(2-α-β)(1-y)^(α+β). Entries sum over parallel edges. Expanding traces therefore gives the boundary enumerator E_B=2^-f tr(T^N). Character orthogonality for JB gives the cycle enumerator E_C=2^-N tr(U^N): the input parametrization has multiplicity 2^f and |JB|=2^(N-f), so the combined normalization is exactly 2^-N. Hence
Q(y)=2^-N tr(U^N)-2^-f tr(T^N)=Σ_(z∈C\B)y^wt(z).
The exponents are ordinary physical Hamming weights on 2N coordinates. All divisions are exact coefficientwise, and Q has nonnegative integer coefficients. If f=0, B=C and Q=0. If f>0, Q(1)=2^(N+f)-2^(N-f)>0, while Q(0)=0; the first positive coefficient occurs at some 1<=d<=2N. Every smaller logical weight is excluded by its zero coefficient.

The X and Z distances agree under J. A nontrivial CSS Pauli has at least one nonboundary X or Z component whose support is contained in its total support, while a pure minimum logical attains this lower bound. Thus this d is the quantum distance, not merely the distance of an auxiliary algebraic code.

For a pin set A_q⊆{0,1} on each physical coordinate, use b_q(s)=1_(s∈A_q)y^s and c_q(s)=Σ_(z∈A_q)(-1)^(sz)y^z. At layer i use boundary weight b_(L,i)(α)b_(R,i)(β) and character weight c_(R,-i)(α)c_(L,-i)(β). The swapped, inverted character positions are forced by J. Their normalized trace difference Q_P counts pinned members of C\B. In particular Q_P=Q_(P,q=0)+Q_(P,q=1). Starting from [y^d]Q>0, pin each coordinate to zero if that branch's coefficient is positive, and otherwise to one. The partition identity preserves positivity. Fully pinned, the count is one and specifies a weight-d nonboundary cycle. Applying J provides a Z witness. As a direct final check, compute its syndrome and use GF(2) elimination to verify that it is outside B.

These derivations check the invoked M6 identities, their domain, normalization, physical metric and pin orientation. No finite stored distance or upper witness substitutes for the lower exclusion.

Every element of the recipe group carries B and C to the corresponding boundary and cycle spaces by a coordinate permutation. For translations use P(u,v)=(x^su,x^tv), whose syndrome is multiplied by x^(s+t); common units use σ_u on both blocks, and exchange swaps blocks. Thus k and d are class invariants and witnesses transport with exactly the same Hamming weight. A presentation change is not treated as an arbitrary module shear.

At w=1 anchoring gives a=b=1. Connectedness then forces N=1. Here f=0 and NoLogical is returned. For N>1 there are no connected w=1 recipes. These cases, and w>N, are exact empty-domain or no-logical decisions, not transfer cutoffs.

5. Actual placements and optimization algorithm.
For c=(A,B), define the finite placement set Π_c=U(N)×{0,1}×(Z/NZ)². A placement p=(u,ε,s,t) realizes ordered supports C_p=uA_ε+s and D_p=uB_ε+t. On the specified cyclic two-rail compiler define δ(i)=min(i,N-i) for residues 0<=i<N and
L(c,p)=Σ_(i∈C_p)δ(i)+Σ_(j∈D_p)δ(j),
R(c,p)=max({δ(i):i∈C_p}∪{δ(j):j∈D_p}).
This generalizes the historical cost definition to every positive w. It is a definition of the compiler, not a claim about another physical layout. Each computed pair has an actual witnessing placement. Since composing group actions permutes Π_c, its set of cost values, and its sector-filtered counterpart, depend only on the recipe class.

Compute k, tagged distance and a minimum witness when one exists for every c∈T. Test K, the class-sector condition, and the distance requirement. For each class passing these tests, traverse Π_c, using its action indices to compute costs, and retain exactly placements satisfying L<=ℓ and R<=r when those caps are present. In placement-sector mode also require τ_u(F_c)∈E. Denote the resulting finite records (c,p) by Z. In class-sector mode retain a separate sector-realizing witness for c.

Evaluate the chosen objective vector z(c,p). Define strict domination for Pareto mode by coordinatewise <= with at least one strict coordinate; equal vectors do not dominate each other. For lexicographic mode use strict lexicographic order. Mark a record winning iff no record of Z strictly dominates it. Return every marked record when all placements are requested, or all marked objective values with one witnessing placement for each (class,value) when compressed output is requested. In either case return every class occurring in a marked record. If distinct physical presentations rather than action records are requested, deduplicate the winning ordered support pairs by exact equality; retain their action witnesses. Distinct actions realizing the same presentation are not asserted to be distinct placements in that output mode.

This construction never uses the possibly unrealizable pair (min L,min R) as an answer. Caps are imposed on actual placements before the front is formed. In particular it does not discard a feasible tradeoff because a different placement separately improves only one locality coordinate.

6. Correctness in both directions; ties and repaired pruning.
Every returned record belongs to Z, so its recipe, exact labels, sector semantics and locality caps pass by construction. Its action realizes the reported supports and costs. Its objective vector has no strict dominator in Z, so it is globally optimal within the declared query.

Conversely, take any connected raw recipe and any feasible placement realizing a globally optimal record. The transversal premise gives its unique c∈T and the group proof gives an invertible action relating it to c. Composing that action with the placement gives an element of Π_c realizing the same ordered supports, costs and physical labels. Signature transport proves that the class-sector condition survives this re-expression; in placement-sector mode the literal placed supports are identical. Therefore the record appears in Z. Its global optimality means it is not marked dominated. Thus its class and objective value are returned, and its physical presentation is returned in all-presentation mode. The same proof applies to every tied record because equality is never strict domination.

If Z is empty, there is no feasible raw recipe/placement, by the same completeness argument. If Z is nonempty, a finite strict Pareto order has a minimal element: following strict dominators cannot repeat a record, since the order is transitive and irreflexive. Lexicographic order has the same property. Hence the winning set is empty exactly when Z is empty.

The correct physical pullback is {g·c : c is returned and the particular action g yields a feasible global-front record}. It is generally a proper subset of the union of returned classes' full orbits. The full orbit union instead describes recipes belonging to classes that possess an optimal placement. These are different assertions.

Optional pruning is sound only with realizable strict domination. A verified lower bound b_c on every feasible objective vector of c permits a whole-class Pareto prune when some feasible record q satisfies z(q)<=b_c with a strict coordinate. Then z(q) strictly dominates every feasible record of c. Equality supplies no such proof. After enumeration, a nonwinning class may require different global-front dominators for different placement values; one witness need not dominate its whole front.

The historical per-block inequality (f',h')<=(f,h) implies only (f'+g,max(h',j))<=(f+g,max(h,j)); strictness can disappear. Therefore block-front reduction can preserve the set of front cost values while losing tied realizing placements. The default algorithm above performs no such reduction. A permitted implementation may compute values using that reduction, then rescan all action indices and restore every feasible placement whose complete objective value lies on the final front. It may not claim all-placement completeness without that restoration. For arbitrary added objectives or predicates, even value reduction requires a separate compatible monotonicity proof.

7. Replayable certificates and terminating verifier.
The certificate contains: the input query; T and Γ if an end-to-end generation certificate is supplied; canonical support pairs and reconstruction actions; F_c and the full unit-transport table; exact k and tagged distance; exact Q coefficients and pin-branch coefficient records; minimum physical witnesses; sector witnesses; and the declared winning records or their precise compressed representation. A nonwinner certificate assigns to each feasible nonwinning record a winning record with a strictly dominating objective vector. A nonwinning class generally has a list of such assignments, not one universal dominator. Rejected classes record the failed class predicate; classes with no feasible placement are certified by the complete placement loop and its failed tests.

The verifier first runs V_gen(Γ,T), when global generation certification is claimed. Without Γ it reports only selection correctness relative to the supplied complete-transversal premise, not certified global completeness. It checks each representative's cardinalities, anchoring, connectedness and κ value, and checks that canonical identifiers are distinct. It recomputes gcds with full multiplicities, every transported signature and each sector witness. It recomputes the M6 trace recurrence through degree 2N, the exact divisions and Q, verifies the first positive coefficient or NoLogical, and replays each pin branch. It independently checks final physical weight, syndrome and nonboundary status by elimination.

Next it runs every declared placement index and recomputes feasibility and objective values. It computes all pairwise strict comparisons, verifies that the supplied winning set is exactly the undominated set, checks every requested tie or its compressed expansion rule, and replays nonwinner dominators. This both excludes a falsely included winner and detects an omitted tied winner. Completeness of this placement loop follows from its explicit finite Cartesian action index set, not from a hash commitment. Empty answers require that this verified feasible-record set be empty, together with V_gen if global emptiness is claimed. The verifier uses only bounded loops, finite polynomial arithmetic and finite elimination, so it terminates. Recomputing intermediate trace arrays is allowed; storing them all is unnecessary.

8. Resources and the no-raw-scan boundary.
Let H=|T|, φ=|U(N)|, b=max(1,ceil(log2(N+1))), P=2φN², Q=|Z|<=HP, and m be the number of objective coordinates. Use the indexed-array bit model of M6 and exact integer arithmetic. The total distance work is O(Σ_c N³4^(R_c)); constructing minimum witnesses for every logical class costs O(Σ_c N⁴4^(R_c)). One transfer calculation uses O(N²2^(R_c)) bits, reusable class by class. The bound follows directly from 2^(R_c) starting states, N layers, at most twice that many edges per layer, O(N) polynomial coefficients and O(N)-bit signed integers. At most 2N extra paired traces perform pinning. Final witness elimination costs O(HN³) bit operations, and coefficient/pin summaries occupy O(HN²) bits.

Canonical validation by sorting all anchor translations for all units costs a conservative O(Hφw² log(w+1)b²) bit operations. Unit enumeration by Euclidean gcd checks costs at most O(Nb³). Computing τ_u(F_c) by modular substitution and binary polynomial Euclid costs O(HφN³) bit operations and O(HφN) bits if the transport table is retained. The explicit signature-list matching adds O(Hφ|E|N) bit operations. No factorization or character-sum cost is hidden in this module: signature testing uses polynomial gcd, while character sums occur in the accounted M6 transfer. The M5 generator's character sums and its coverage ledger have an additional, currently unspecified cost C_gen outside this theorem.

For each unit and swap, precompute each block's sums and maxima for all N shifts in O(Nwb²) bit work. Combining them over (s,t) costs O(N²b²). Total locality evaluation is O(Hφ(Nw+N²)b²), plus exact query evaluation. With default objective-coordinate bit length B=O(b), pairwise front verification costs O(Q²mB), and storing records costs O(Q(m+1)B) bits. Longer input integers add their actual comparison bit lengths. Signature preprocessing can be streamed to trade storage for recomputation.

Outputting O_out action records costs at least Ω(O_out b) bits; explicitly expanding their supports and transported physical witnesses adds O(O_out(w+N)b) work by a conservative bound. Directly rechecking every expanded witness by elimination, if requested, adds O(O_out N³). Overall workspace can be bounded by O(max_c N²2^(R_c)+HN²+HφN+Q(m+1)B), excluding the generator certificate and final expanded output. Replay has the same listed principal costs, plus the independent cost of V_gen.

The selector traverses H supplied classes and at most HP placement action records. It does not generate candidate support pairs from the Cartesian domain of size binom(N,w)², and it does not canonicalize placement records as new candidate classes. Placement costs can be computed from the precomputed block arrays without materializing the corresponding ordered support pairs. Nevertheless HP can be very large; if all physical presentations are requested, output expansion can itself contain a large fraction of the raw domain. This cost is explicit, and no practical superiority or arbitrary-span polynomial bound is claimed. Most importantly, this module does not prove that T was obtained without a raw scan: that is exactly the complementary generator obligation.

9. Acceptance boundary and falsifiers.
The assigned mathematical selection-module criterion is met under the stated complete-transversal premise: labels cover all legal orders and multiplicities; feasibility and both optimization directions are proved; sector witnesses and all-placement semantics are explicit; ties and empty queries are handled; and verification and costs are specified. The universal theorem is symbolic, not a finite computational certificate or an executed implementation result.

Original M7's exact gate is 'All certified optima for requested parameters without raw scan.' This sublemma supplies the labels, constraints, optimality and placement reconstruction after certified generation. It does not close the no-raw-scan generation clause, integrate that generator into an unchanged complete artifact, or supply the requested fresh dual reviews. M5 is used only through a future generation interface, not assumed to prove that interface automatically; M6 is used only at its checked exact-reduction strength. M8, practical superiority and Lean are not additional gates.

Concrete falsifiers for a realization include: a missing or duplicate canonical class; a failed reconstruction action; an incorrect τ_u(F), particularly at repeated factors; canonical-only rejection of an orbit meeting E; nonintegral normalized traces; negative Q or pinned counts; a smaller positive logical coefficient than the reported d; a witness of wrong physical weight, nonzero syndrome or boundary membership; assigning numerical infinity to NoLogical; an unrealizable reported locality point; a lost equal-cost placement or class; a nonwinner lacking strict pointwise domination; and an empty answer despite a feasible record. The complete-transversal falsifiers belong to the generator interface; the remaining falsifiers are excluded by this module's proof and checked by its verifier.
